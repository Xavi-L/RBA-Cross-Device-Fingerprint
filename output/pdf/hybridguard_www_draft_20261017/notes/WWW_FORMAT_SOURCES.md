# WWW format basis

Checked 17 October 2026. The user did not specify a year; this draft uses the published **WWW 2027 Research Track long-paper** requirements as its formatting target.

Official source: [WWW 2027 Research Track Papers](https://www2027.thewebconf.org/research-track-papers/).

- English, two columns, ACM `acmart`; recommended class options: `sigconf, anonymous, review`.
- Main text: at most 8 pages. References and optional appendix: at most 12 PDF pages overall. The main text must stand alone.
- Double-blind submission: omit author identities and acknowledgments; discuss self-citations in the third person.
- An ethics/consent section is encouraged where appropriate. Institutional human-participant policies apply; report real IRB review where applicable, never an invented approval.
- Current abstract/full-paper deadlines are 18/25 October 2026, AoE. Formatting this draft does not register or submit it.
- Authors remain accountable for AI-assisted text; an LLM cannot be an author.

This is a writing and layout draft, not a certification of submission readiness. Actual submission also requires author decisions, complete evidence review, anonymized artifact access, and compliance with the then-current conference and institutional policies. These notes do not establish those facts.
