\subsection{Saved Model Identities}\label{app:1}}

App configurations 01, 02, and 03 hold out emulator API 29, 30, and 36, respectively. APP\_\allowbreak{}FULL denotes the retained B\_\allowbreak{}REL\_\allowbreak{}TZ models, called Integrated App in the main text. A\_\allowbreak{}NO\_\allowbreak{}MTC\_\allowbreak{}CAP, A\_\allowbreak{}APP\_\allowbreak{}WEB\_\allowbreak{}ONLY, A\_\allowbreak{}NO\_\allowbreak{}MEMORY\_\allowbreak{}REL, and A\_\allowbreak{}NO\_\allowbreak{}TIMEZONE\_\allowbreak{}REL are refitted alternatives. APP\_\allowbreak{}TREE uses saved FITTED outputs; rule models use saved RETENTION outputs. PAIRED\_\allowbreak{}BASE is the frozen App detector plus C1. These names do not add Host geometry to Integrated App.

The candidate-state tree uses scikit-learn 1.7.2, splitter=best, random\_\allowbreak{}state=20261006, no class-weight override, and a positive-class probability threshold of 0.5. Its admitted pools contain 63, 63, and 54 atoms, each encoded by three state indicators; these are smaller than the pre-admission pools. Fifteen positions in each controlled held-out set and 73/891 MTC records have an unknown candidate. A fitted path need not use that candidate, so binary predictions do not imply complete observation.

\hypertarget{app:2}{%
\subsection{Candidate Admission and Extension Choice}\label{app:2}}

Threshold expansion uses training-only quantiles 0.25, 0.5, and 0.75. Candidate support requires at least three complete true/false triplets and at least two triggered modification triplets in at least one bundle/environment, alongside normal assessability. For Browser extension, feasible choices among none, C1, C2, and C1+C2 are ranked by the equal mean detection ratio across App timezone, App language, Browser timezone, and Browser language. Ties prefer fewer additions and then a fixed set order. This ranking is part of development-side selection.

The fixed timezone comparator triggers above −480 minutes; the old Web-height comparator triggers above 710 CSS pixels. Native timezone uses effective offsets under the fixed implementation database. Geometry follows Section~\ref{sec:3-3}. C1 requires both status and quality markers, accepts finite numeric offsets including zero and minus one, and applies no timestamp gate.

\hypertarget{app:4}{%
\subsection{Verification Scope}\label{app:4}}

Presentation checks verify state partitions, aggregate/detail agreement, row counts, figure and table references, and file hashes. They establish consistency between saved results and the manuscript, without independently validating intervention labels, source-record identity, or collection bindings. The implementation also contains tests for decision states and binding failures. Those tests are distinct from the benchmark outcomes reported here.

\hypertarget{app:5}{%
\subsection{Implementation and Comparator Provenance}\label{app:5}}

The implementation archive provides the 50 base templates and three relations in \path|deliverables/app177_core_ablation_v1/CANDIDATES.json|, with their dependencies in \texttt{CANDIDATE\_\allowbreak{}DEPENDENCIES.md}. Final B\_\allowbreak{}REL\_\allowbreak{}TZ models are saved under \path|deliverables/timezone_relation_validation_v1/trials/B_REL_TZ__WEBGL1-LOEO-v1-{01,02,03}__RETENTION/model.json|. All three select Native--Web timezone and memory relations, MIME type count above zero, the first-language relation (\texttt{RSR-LANG-FIRST-v1}), and the registered UA/platform condition (\texttt{CAT:NW-006}). Configurations 01/02 also select Web inner height above 710 CSS pixels; configuration 03 does not. The resulting models have 6/6/5 clauses and complexity 12/12/10.

The active selector, \path|hybridguard_agent/research/rule_learning_v2/semantic_selection.py|, ranks feasible retention proposals by higher macro-averaged detection gain, higher semantic-coverage gain, fewer controlled-normal alarms, higher minimum phase coverage, lower complexity, higher semantic quality, and finally a structural key. The six saved B\_\allowbreak{}REL\_\allowbreak{}TZ fits terminate through their heuristic stopping criteria, with recorded elapsed times of 0.19--0.48 seconds. The 60-second deadline is applied to each fit and is not their recorded stopping reason.

Record-level outputs are retained in \path|deliverables/timezone_relation_validation_v1/predictions.jsonl.gz|, \path|deliverables/app177_core_ablation_v1/results/{full_predictions,predictions}.jsonl.gz|, and \path|deliverables/cross_endpoint_constrained_extension_v1/results/predictions.jsonl|. These archives support inspection beyond the aggregate tables; saved outputs and independently reproduced results remain distinct forms of evidence.

Comparator provenance differs across studies. The 8 GiB condition was fixed in the memory study's \texttt{SETTINGS.json} before the 72-position collection that yielded 18 effective modifications and 48 normal positions; comparisons on the older controlled and MTC records were retrospective. The legacy 710 CSS-pixel height condition appears in the geometry study's \texttt{CONDITIONS\_\allowbreak{}FROZEN.json}, fixed before the formal 72-position batch and after six engineering smoke observations. The −480-minute comparator is the legacy absolute-offset condition from the saved B\_\allowbreak{}REL models; old and new model identities were frozen before the 36-position timezone collection. We treat these as controlled mechanism studies within development, without claiming independently preregistered comparator selection. The memory envelope and geometry tolerance are engineering hypotheses within their stated domains, and no threshold-sensitivity study is reported.
