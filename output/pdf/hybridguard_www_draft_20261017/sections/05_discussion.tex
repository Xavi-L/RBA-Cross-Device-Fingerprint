\section{Discussion, Limitations, and Ethics}
\label{sec:limits}
\paragraph{What the evidence establishes.}
Cross-layer references can add information that selected single-field checks lack, and can also explain why an ordinary setting change should not alarm. Their value is conditional on available, correctly interpreted measurements. The strongest conclusion is therefore about specific mechanisms and their tradeoffs. The observations do not establish an independently trusted layer or a theoretical guarantee of detecting inconsistent manipulation.

\paragraph{Generality and attribution.}
The controlled benchmark covers three emulator environments and a finite set of recipes. Its repeated stages and development exposure limit independence. MTC normal records broaden the observed settings but do not supply attacks on an independent sample of physical devices. They are not clean-device attestations, and their historical split names do not erase development exposure. An ablation retrains the remaining pool, so its difference measures the behavior of that entire constrained alternative; it is not a causal estimate for arbitrary devices. The tree comparison controls representation and capacity but is not a comparison with tuned modern classifiers. No adaptive adversarial optimization or calibrated operational risk assessment is evaluated.

\paragraph{Missingness, drift, and integration.}
Unknown output is an operational cost, not a successful rejection or a harmless missing column. Adding a reference can turn a definite no-alert result into unknown, as seen with C1. Changes in WebView providers, API exposure, locale conventions, or device coverage may invalidate admissibility assumptions. The geometry check needs integrated selection and real-device validation, including window modes, keyboard interaction, and padding cases. C1 has no timestamp gate: collection-time differences can be benign, so its observed 0/46 normal alarms do not establish general robustness to asynchronous setting changes. Neither all 177 App fields nor all 244 paired fields have demonstrated marginal utility.

\paragraph{Privacy and responsible use.}
Collecting more device information to assess integrity can itself enable tracking. WebView tracking research and W3C guidance make that cost explicit~\cite{weerasekara2025tracking,w3c2025fingerprinting}. The reported manipulations concern controlled emulator environments; historical normals come from a cloud-device collection process. Its documented App permission scope is network access and network-state observation, without requesting contacts, SMS, photos, location, camera, or microphone access. This bounds that collection request, not all possible privacy implications of the fingerprint.

The draft artifact provides derived counts, condition definitions, and table sources; it does not bundle raw device payloads, pair tickets, or persistent identifiers. Institutional review or exemption and participant-consent approval are not established by the examined study artifacts, and we make no such claim. Those requirements and any raw-data release basis must be resolved through the applicable institutional process. A future deployment should minimize retained information and provide a fallback for unknown or conflicting evidence; these results do not justify automatic denial of service or authentication based solely on a fingerprint alert.
