\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:limits}
\subsection{What Counts as Integrity Evidence?}
The reference benefits in Section~\ref{sec:eval_references} concern contextual compatibility under the applicability conditions in Section~\ref{sec:setting}. A violated relation motivates scrutiny of report integrity without identifying which observation was altered or establishing malicious intent. Normal changes, sampling times, and implementation differences require interpretation within those conditions before a discrepancy can support an attack hypothesis. Conversely, modified observations can remain compatible, so an absent trigger does not establish authenticity.

The evidence comes from finite recipes in three emulator environments; repeated runs and development exposure limit independence. MTC normals broaden observed normal conditions without supplying independent physical-device attack generalization. Retrained ablations characterize entire constrained alternatives, not single-factor causal effects for arbitrary devices. The fixed-capacity tree is likewise not a comprehensive comparison with tuned advanced classifiers.

\subsection{When Cross-Layer Consistency Fails}
Historical field availability, lost webdriver-false semantics, and assessability admission limit the candidates for webdriver and WebGL, revealing representation gaps rather than intrinsic undetectability. The tested language candidate C2 exceeded the normal-alarm budget on legitimate preference changes. These findings limit the tested candidates, without establishing that language observations lack information.

Other modifications can remain within a checked relation. With valid operands, an altered Web memory value at or below the Native-derived envelope does not trigger that condition. This follows from its predicate; the experiments do not cover all downward or within-envelope modifications.

Unavailable or ambiguous required evidence, or unmet applicability conditions, leave a relation unassessed. The states defined in Sections~\ref{sec:setting} and~\ref{sec:method} distinguish this from an observed no-alert result or an execution failure. Current misses help locate unmet conditions, but establish neither necessary-and-sufficient detection conditions nor an automatic benefit from more observations.

\subsection{Adaptive and Coordinated Adversaries}
Even with usable evidence, reports preserving the checked relations may be indistinguishable from compatible normal observations under those checks. Native and Host are not trusted roots; different observation positions imply neither statistical nor administrative independence. Evasion depends on the selected checks' actual dependencies, without necessarily requiring control of all three surfaces or all fields. Some local modifications may remain compatible without changes elsewhere.

Adaptive adversarial optimization and comprehensive coordinated attacks have not been evaluated. This threat analysis supplies neither bypass rates nor evidence of increased attacker cost. Consistency cannot substitute for trusted attestation.

\subsection{Deployment, Drift, and Missingness}
Changes in WebView providers, API exposure, system versions, locale conventions, container behavior, or device coverage may alter measurement semantics and applicability. These are potential drift risks requiring longitudinal and cross-version validation. Observation association is not atomic sampling. C1 has no timestamp gate, and low alarms on the paired normals do not establish general robustness to asynchronous setting changes.

Adding references can increase inconclusive outputs, as C1 does on historical normals. Unknown cannot automatically mean normal, attack, or authentication failure. Deployment would require designed and validated handling of insufficient or conflicting evidence; automatic fallback, calibrated risk, and an authentication decision process are not established by this study.

Integrating the Host geometry specialist would require joint evaluation of detection, normal alarms, availability, and complexity. Real devices, window modes, keyboard interaction, and padding need further validation; the current zero-padding domain remains unchanged. Nor has independent incremental value been demonstrated for every one of the 177 App or 244 paired fields.

\subsection{Privacy and Ethics}
Collecting environment information for integrity checking can also enable identification, linkage, and tracking, as discussed in WebView research and W3C guidance~\cite{weerasekara2025tracking,w3c2025fingerprinting}. Controlled manipulations used emulators; historical normals came from cloud-device collection. The documented MTC collector requested network access and network-state observation, without contacts, SMS, photos, location, camera, or microphone permissions. Limited permissions remove neither fingerprinting privacy risks nor the need to determine applicable review and authorization requirements.

The paper package supplies derived counts, condition definitions, and table sources, without raw device payloads, pair tickets, or stable device identifiers; this scope does not extend to the repository as a whole. Institutional review, exemption, and participant-consent status are not established by the available study documentation.

Future use should minimize retained observations and handle uncertain evidence cautiously. The experiments do not justify automatic denial of service, rejection of identity, or high-stakes security decisions based solely on a fingerprint alert.
