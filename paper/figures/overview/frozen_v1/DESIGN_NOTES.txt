ENGLISH CAPTION
Complementary observation surfaces for cross-checking Android environment
reports. The solid blue Native–App Web edge denotes memory and timezone
consistency relations used by the current detector. Host–web geometry is
implemented and evaluated separately, outside the current Full App detector
and its candidate pool. The dotted Native–Host edge denotes shared context
only, not an independent detection rule. Open arrowheads indicate comparisons,
not communication paths or mutual authentication. Association within the same
App execution does not imply atomic sampling, and no surface is trusted ground
truth. Single-view checks, the optional linked Browser, and the complete
decision process appear in the system-design figure.

SEMANTIC BOUNDARIES
The triangle introduces observation surfaces and cross-view relations; it is
not a complete list of the current detector's checks. Full App also includes
single-view checks. The three edges do not represent a symmetric three-way
validation or voting loop and provide no trusted-reference guarantee.

Memory checking uses an admissible upper envelope, not raw-value equality.
Timezone checking accounts for representation and effective-offset semantics.
Native–web consistency therefore describes relation checking; it does not
claim that all reported values are directly equal across views.

Separate study does not mean unimplemented, planned future work, or an optional
switch in the current detector. The Native–Host dotted connection is not an
independent detection rule; contextual information may still be relevant to
other selected checks.
