## Original technical pictograms

`color-memory.svg`, `color-clock.svg`, `color-dimensions.svg`, `color-host.svg`,
`color-phone.svg`, `color-browser.svg`, and `mismatch-symbol.svg` are original
editable vector geometry authored for Figure 1 v4 in the generator cited above.
They are reused here with the original paths and presentation colors.

The Host and browser-window pictograms are generic technical illustrations,
not official WebView or browser product marks. The clock is a schematic cue.
The red mismatch cross denotes a depicted relation outcome, not a claim about
the entire detector or device. No app or external-browser brand artwork is
included in this asset selection.

Each file contains vector shapes only. There are no visible text nodes, embedded
raster images, linked fonts, or external dependencies. Internal identifiers may
be namespaced by the Figure 3 assembler without altering the artwork.
