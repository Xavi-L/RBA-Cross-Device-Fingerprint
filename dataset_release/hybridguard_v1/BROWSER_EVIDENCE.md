The pilot comprises Browser language and timezone controls with three repetitions and clean/active/restored observations. The matched study comprises seven scenarios with two repetitions and pre/change/post observations, including legitimate system and Browser preference changes. Its 42 canonical positions come from 48 recorded attempts: a predeclared policy replaces exactly six original Browser-language-preference positions with six repair positions. The superseded original attempts are preserved at their source and are excluded from this release. Selection is not based on detector outcomes.

The existing 18-position Browser pilot snapshot is another representation of the same pilot observations. It contributes no additional independent records. The two persisted MTC snapshot versions likewise share all 1,028 paired sample identities. Those MTC observations are a separate historical source; their source snapshots must not be added to the 891 paper representatives as new independent devices. The linked study and the frozen MTC raw archive share no App session identities.

The export adapter reads original physical-line references, verifies completed pair bindings against both endpoint archives and capture records, and preserves actual three-phase relationships. It compares the pilot snapshot with the original 244 recorded values. For the 14 recorded interventions it checks that the target fields changed to the recorded target and returned to the pre-intervention values; the non-target endpoint operands remain unchanged. Existing operation qualification is retained separately from saved detector outputs. This extraction performs no new collection, attacks, model fitting, or detector execution.

Saved predictions contain three model outputs for each position. These produce 180 output rows, not 180 observations. Their states remain annotations and never become input features or intervention labels. A normal control denotes the documented experimental condition, including legitimate changes; it is not a general assertion that a device is benign.

All 60 positions were used during extension development. They comprise correlated observations from two emulator environments and two collector installations, not 60 independent physical devices. No independent train/validation/test split is asserted. The source pilot's candidate/run-scoped identity remains unchanged. Asynchronous legitimate timezone changes are outside the tested condition. The 14 interventions and 46 normal positions match the paper's linked Browser denominator, while the three saved model configurations reuse those same positions.

Raw captures contain session identities, install identifiers, receipts, exact times, temporary Browser tickets, infrastructure addresses, and detailed fingerprint values. Raw logs and source mappings stay in private storage. The project lead's public authorization covers this selected scope. The Reviewer Release excludes raw captures, source paths and source hashes, retains the existing sanitized fields, and documents residual fingerprint linkage risk.
