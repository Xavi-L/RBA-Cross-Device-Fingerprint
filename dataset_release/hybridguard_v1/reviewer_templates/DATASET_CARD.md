# Dataset card — Cross-View Consistency Dataset v1.0

## Purpose and release status

The dataset supports inspection of cross-view consistency, controlled target effects, field availability and recorded mechanism studies across Android Native, WebView Host, App Web and linked Browser Web contexts. This release includes all 1,509 selected observations. Public authorization for all included sources, including MTC, has been confirmed by the project lead. The data reuse license is pending explicit selection; this card does not grant additional rights. No new collection, fitting, detector evaluation or attack execution is performed to create this release.

## Population and dependence

Controlled and specialist studies comprise repeated observations in API 29, 30 and 36 emulator environments. The linked Browser development study uses two recorded emulator/Browser contexts. Historical MTC observations are frozen manufacturer/model/Android-release representatives; transitive grouping uses model, installation and session evidence. These groups are not verified physical-device identities. Selection is not a probability sample of Android users or devices.

There are 558 App177 and 951 Paired244 observations. The latter include 60 linked Browser development observations and 891 historical MTC representatives. Nonrepresentative MTC observations, separate App-only/partial/repeated routing classes and duplicate snapshots are outside the selected research cohort. They are not newly added observations. Historical MTC discovery, development and reserved counts are 630, 144 and 117, with 610, 142 and 116 groups respectively. Global quality checks and earlier diagnostics preceded those historical assignments. There is no new leakage-free split and no established independent-device count.

## Labels and evidence

The 618 controlled, specialist and linked observations have labels verified against recorded evidence: 170 modified, 442 normal controls and six no-effect operations. The checks reconcile stored membership, endpoint bindings, target values, intervention qualification and restoration evidence. They are not an independent physical experiment or replication. Saved detector outputs are separate annotations and do not determine intervention labels.

All 891 MTC observations remain `unlabeled`, with unverified intervention labels. A collection context without a declared intervention does not establish a benign ground-truth class. `normal_control` denotes a supported experimental control, including legitimate preference or system changes, rather than comprehensive benignness. The six memory no-effect operations remain separate from both modifications and the paper's normal-control denominator.

## Structure, availability and privacy

App177 has 84 Native, 26 WebView Host and 67 App Web slots. Paired244 adds an actual 67-field Browser endpoint. Original field structure is complete for 1,506 observations. Three selected MTC App payloads each lack five original fields; all 15 absent cells remain explicitly absent. No Browser or missing measurement is synthesized.

Each cell separates original field presence, collection status, value and privacy disposition. Observed, unsupported, error, timeout, permission denied, not applicable and missing states remain distinct. Legacy sentinels do not automatically become missing. The original fractional viewport-scale measurements retain their values under a numeric type correction; other integer fields are not broadly widened.

Release IDs are random. Private mappings, source identities, precise times, high-entropy/free-text fingerprints, raw credentials, original-source locators and source-digest inventories are excluded. Numeric/boolean values and validated language/timezone/geometry semantics are retained. There are 72 observations with separately recorded geometry supplements. Pair/group IDs retain 206 experiment triplets and 951 real linked endpoints.

Retained feature combinations, labels and grouping still permit within-release joins and may support external linkage or reidentification. Removal of direct identities and a clean pattern scan do not prove anonymity against every adversary. Institutional-review or exemption details were not independently established by the packaging task and are not asserted by this card.

## Supported and unsupported conclusions

The supplied validator reproduces counts, feature-availability/redaction statistics and relationship checks from released files. The artifact supports analyses of retained values, controlled mechanisms and known missingness within the observed scope.

It does not support claims of broad real-world attack prevalence, unseen-device/tool generalization, 1,509 independent devices or independent replication across reused environments. It cannot fully reproduce the original detector inputs or all reported scores, because some intervened-upon or decision-relevant fields are suppressed. Do not treat unlabeled MTC observations as supervised negatives, infer absent Browser values or multiply saved model/fold outputs into new observations.
