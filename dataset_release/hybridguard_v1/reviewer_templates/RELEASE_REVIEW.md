# Release scope and remaining decisions

All 1,509 selected observations have project-lead public authorization. The authorization includes controlled experiments, specialist experiments, Linked Browser and MTC. Source ownership and third-party publication authorization are not pending items for this artifact.

The scope preserves 558 App177 and 951 Paired244 observations, 618 labels verified against recorded evidence, 891 unlabeled MTC observations and three structurally incomplete observations. Existing random IDs, source groups, pairings and privacy suppression remain in place. The artifact makes no claim of independent physical-device counts, new experimental replication or complete reproduction of original detector scores.

The archive includes standalone loading and validation scripts. Original research sources are unnecessary for reading all released observations and recomputing the provided statistics. Private identity mappings, raw captures, credentials, internal logs, original-source paths/digests, author contact details and repository history are excluded by the release file policy.

The remaining human choices are:

1. Select the dataset license and final attribution wording. CC BY 4.0 is the recommended option when attribution is desired; CC0 is an alternative requiring a deliberate choice. Neither is applied by this artifact. See `LICENSE_OPTIONS.md`.
2. Confirm the applicable license for the bundled scripts separately from the data license. The package does not invent a new software license.
3. Select the hosting and review-sharing mode, and inspect the actual signed-out landing page, displayed metadata and download. A local package validation cannot inspect a future hosting page.

Retained field combinations and groups may link observations to other materials. Automated checks establish only the stated content and consistency conditions; they do not establish irreversible anonymity. No hosting upload or publication is performed by the supplied scripts.
